# Plan

The brief is in `BRIEF.md`. Each row is a decision taken here, and the words of the brief it answers.

| # | Decision | From the brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Builder project: every rule in `crates/core` (Rust, Q16.16 integers, no floats or clocks), a shim that only translates, a static page that draws. Ribbit King (`NickHGame`) is the base: its renderer (instanced boxes with an inked outline pass), transport, art pipeline, checks and Pages workflow. | "use the Builder repo as a base"; "use the work and repos of Sam gilson" |
| P2 | Online: Ribbit King's WebRTC transport unchanged (Trystero room codes over public Nostr relays, or pasted invitations; star through the host). On top of it, GGPO-style rollback instead of Ribbit King's host tick stream: every browser runs the whole run, uses its own input after a 2-tick delay, predicts others by repeating their last input, and re-runs ticks from a snapshot when a real input differs (`rollback.rs`). At most 14 ticks ahead of the last fully known tick. Copies hash confirmed ticks every 120 ticks; the host compares. | "use the multiplayer from ribbit king … try and make it rollback" |
| P3 | Third-person over-the-shoulder camera, pointer-locked mouse look, aim zoom set by the sight; arrow keys also turn. Shots leave from the muzzles of the gun's own model and converge on what the crosshair's ray meets. | "a 3rd person shooter"; "fire their shots out of the 3d rendered end of the gun" |
| P4 | Six part categories of five (30 parts, `data/parts.json`): frame (base), barrel (the damaging piece), mount (how many barrels and where: single, side-by-side, over-under, square, gatling ring), payload (what each shot carries: slug, blast, goo, shock, ember), sight, grip. Composition in `gun.rs`. A square mount of double barrels gives eight muzzles; an AK frame with a rocket tube and goo fires goo rockets that slow and eat armour for no damage. | "different base parts to your weapon, accessories, handles, and the damaging pieces"; the two example guns; "5 of each gun making piece" |
| P5 | Extraction: the stash lives in the browser (localStorage). A part on your gun leaves the shelf; if you do not come back in the van it is lost (basic parts never run out). Everything in your pack when the van leaves goes to the shelf; a filled order pays rare parts. Dying drops your pack where you fell for teammates to pick up. | "all gear you pick up during the run gets extracted like arc raiders" |
| P6 | One map, built in code (`mall.rs`): a two-level concourse with balconies and bridges, centre court with escalators, food court, three department stores, a west wing, a side hall out to an outdoor brick Main Street with a cinema, and parking lots with poles, dead cars and trees to the edge, the interstate beyond. Five entrances, one van spot each, 100–180 m out. | "one map based on the southpoint mall … including the outside streets"; "far in the parking lot"; "several entrances" |
| P7 | The run starts seated in the back of the van with the doors shut; hold E to open them (they open by themselves after 12 s). The van is also the way out: hold E inside to start a 10 s countdown; whoever is in it when it goes comes back, everyone else is left. | "start the mission in the back of your van like phasmophobia" |
| P8 | Six drones (`data/drones.json`, `drone.rs`, `ai.rs`). Flyers: wasp, moth, hornet, warden. Walkers: tick, strider. Each has hit spheres for body, armour (most of a hit soaked; goo eats it), a glowing weak spot, and rotors that can be shot off. Hits push and spin flyers; they lean into acceleration and spring back; a lost rotor drags them down; half the rotors gone and they fall. | "4 drone types that fly, and 2 that walk like spiders"; "weak spots and strong spots"; "move like real life drones when shot" |
| P9 | Behaviour trees as data (`ai.rs`): selector/sequence/condition/action, ticked every 4 ticks. Wasps patrol, approach and circle-strafe; hornets duck into cover when looked at, peek out somewhere new and charge a telegraphed laser; moths spot, call nearby drones, and bait a raider in cover by showing themselves then drifting back toward wasps told to wait in ambush; wardens hold range with the armour forward; ticks rush and detonate; striders keep distance, strafe, fire and stomp. Drones hear shots. | "behavior trees to control when they peek you, how they hover, how they circle you … bait you out of cover" |
| P10 | Five orders (`data/missions.json`): The Shirt (Hot Topic, fetch), Pest Control (clear the food court nest), Footage (three drives), Nest Breaker (break the hive's pods then its core), Lights On (start the generator and hold the court through waves). Each starts at a different entrance. | "5 different missions"; "find an old rare kid cudi shirt in an old hot topic"; "clear the mall" |
| P11 | Downed, not dead: at 0 health a raider crawls and bleeds out over 30 s; a teammate holds E for 3 s to revive. Everyone down ends the run. | Arc Raiders' co-op |
| P12 | 2D art in TikZ (`tools/art.py` and its three modules), after Sam_Comm_final and the other games' menus: title, five order postcards, six drone portraits, thirty part icons. | "use the tikz latex prompt … sam comm final as an inspiration image" |
